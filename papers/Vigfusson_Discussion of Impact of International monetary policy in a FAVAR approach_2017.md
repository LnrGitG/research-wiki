# Are we calculating the relevant object? 

- Much of the discussion here and in the literature has studied the effects of 

   - "all-else-equal" monetary policy actions (i.e. in isolation) . 

- But, frequently, monetary policy is reacting to other events. 

- As a result, the effects of monetary policy in isolation can be more than offset by the effects of these other events. 

- As highlighted, in Ammer, De Pooter, Erceg, and Kamin (2016), the important question is whether monetary policy is stabilizing.

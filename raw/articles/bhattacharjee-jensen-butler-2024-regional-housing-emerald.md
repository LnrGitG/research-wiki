# Bhattacharjee & Jensen-Butler (2024) — A model of regional housing markets in England and Wales

**Journal:** Asian Journal of Economics and Banking 8(2): 146–161  
**Open Access:** CC BY 4.0  
**URL (PDF):** https://www.emerald.com/ajeb/article-pdf/8/2/146/9598654/ajeb-03-2024-0024.pdf

## Research question
Can a micro-founded model close macro housing markets (demand-supply-search-price) and capture regional heterogeneity + spatial diffusion?

## Methodology

### Macro micro-foundation
- Demand, supply and prices linked through micro model of **search, matching and price formation**
- Vacancy rates unobservable → demand-supply mismatch identified via matching friction
- Macro-micro bridge: closing macro models when price/vacancy/quantity data incomplete

### Econometric methodology
- Empirical model estimated for **10 Government Office Regions** of England + Wales
- New generation econometric methods for empirical analysis
- Captures unrestricted inter-regional spatial relationships
- **Spatial autocorrelation** estimated separately per region → different drivers of spatial diffusion

## Key findings
1. Substantial heterogeneity across 10 regions (policy implication for UK land use)
2. Spatial autocorrelations vary by region → implies **region-specific diffusion drivers** (not uniform spillover)
3. Model validates economic theory: macro-micro closing works with incomplete data

## Contribution
Methodological template for closing macro housing models — transferable to Russian regional market where price/volume data are also incomplete (Rosstat vs developer data discrepancies).

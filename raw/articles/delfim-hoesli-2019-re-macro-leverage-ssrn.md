# Delfim & Hoesli (2019) — Real Estate Performance, the Macroeconomy and Leverage

**Swiss Finance Institute Research Paper No. 19-33**  
**Authors:** Jean-Christophe Delfim, Martin Hoesli (University of Geneva / Swiss Finance Institute / Aberdeen)  
**Sample:** U.S. 1986–2017

## Research question
How do macroeconomic risk factors and financial leverage affect performance across RE exposure types (direct, non-listed REITs, listed REITs)?

## Methodology

### VAR framework
- **Vector Autoregression** with macroeconomic factors + RE performance measures
- IRFs (impulse response functions) for GDP, inflation, interest rates, unemployment shocks
- Compared to stock-market shocks for listed REITs

### Leverage channel
- Decomposition of RE returns into unlevered + leveraged components
- Leverage amplifies responses to economic factors → higher investment risk
- Tests across strategy types: core funds vs value-added vs opportunistic

## Key findings
1. **Core funds ≈ direct investments** in macroeconomic response (low beta to stocks; high beta to rates/GDP)
2. **Aggressive strategies (value-added, opportunistic):** decoupled from direct investment performance
3. **Listed REITs only:** strongly linked to stock market
4. **Leverage amplifies** macro responses → higher risk for levered exposure
5. **Mortgage rate shock** transmits to all RE types but with different magnitude/latency

## Methodological template
VAR + leverage decomposition — applicable to Russian REITs (when developed) and highly-leveraged developers (ПИК, Самолёт).

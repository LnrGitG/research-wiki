# Yunus (2012) — Modeling Relationships Among Securitized Property Markets, Stock Markets, and Macroeconomic Variables

**Journal of Real Estate Research 34(2), 2012**  
**Author:** Nafeesa Yunus (University of Baltimore)  
**Sample:** 10 developed nations (North America, Europe, Australia, Asia)  
**Indices:** EPRA (Europe), NAREIT (US/Australia/Asia)

## Research question
Dynamic interactions between securitized RE markets, stock markets, and macro factors in the long run (co-integration) and short run (shock responses)?

## Methodology

### Unit root tests
- ADF, PP, KPSS on time series
- All variables in logs (except rates)

### Co-integration
- **Johansen co-integration analysis** for long-run relationships
- Each national RE market co-integrated with its stock market + macro factors

### Vector Error Correction Model (VECM)
- Short-run dynamics: how deviation from long-run equilibrium corrected
- IRFs for shocks to GDP, M3, inflation, long-term rates, stock markets

## Key findings
1. **Co-integration confirmed:** each country's securitized RE market tied to its macro variables long-run
2. **Short-run influence:** macro shocks propagate to RE returns with sign/direction:
   - GDP ↑ → RE returns ↑
   - M3 (money supply) ↑ → RE returns ↑
   - Inflation ↑ → RE returns ↑ (mostly)
   - Long-term rate ↑ → RE returns ↓
   - Stock market shocks → RE returns ↑ (positive correlation)
3. **Response magnitude differs** significantly across 10 countries (institutional/market structure)

## Methodological relevance
VECM template for cross-country RE comparison — directly applicable to Russia vs OECD comparison with EPRA/REIT-equivalent indices (when available).

Seasonal Adjustment: Seasonally Adjusted
Coverage: United States (national + 50 states + metros)

- Total: 51 series in this release

ZHVI Definition:
- Smoothed, seasonally adjusted measure of typical home value
- Covers homes in 35th-65th percentile range
- Includes: single-family residences, condos, co-ops
- Updated monthly down to ZIP code and neighborhood level
- Disseminated through FRED alongside government series

Key Series (National):
- USAUCSFRCONDOSMSAMID: All homes (SFR + condos + co-ops) - US national
- Latest value (Jun 2026): $372,995
- Jan 2000 value: $125,207
- Cumulative growth (2000-2026): ~198% (≈4.3% annualized)

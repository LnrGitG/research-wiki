# Bank of Russia Annual Report for 2021

## Abstract (key passage on housing and monetary policy)

The annual report discusses the interaction between monetary policy and housing markets in the context of post-pandemic recovery (2021). Key points relevant to the research cluster:

### Monetary Policy Stance and Housing

The report notes that accommodative monetary policy during the pandemic supported credit markets, including mortgage lending. However, this created challenges:

1. **Mortgage market expansion:** Low interest rates (key rate cut to 4.25% in July 2020) combined with subsidized mortgage programs led to rapid growth in mortgage lending.

2. **Housing price dynamics:** The report observes that faster rise in housing prices could wipe out gains from interest rate cuts. This creates a tension: monetary easing aimed at supporting the economy through lower borrowing costs may be offset by higher house prices, reducing affordability.

3. **Financial stability risks:** The rapid expansion of mortgage lending raised concerns about potential risks to financial stability. The Bank of Russia began implementing macroprudential measures to contain these risks.

### Policy Response

The report discusses the Bank of Russia's approach to balancing:
- Supporting economic recovery through accommodative monetary policy
- Preventing overheating in housing and credit markets
- Using macroprudential tools as complement to monetary policy

### Relevance to Research Cluster

This report provides context for the Russian experience (2020-2021 period) discussed in:
- Demidova & Shchankina (2025) — transmission broke during COVID period
- Sinyakov & Shelovanova (2023/2025) — weak interest rate elasticity
- Smirnova (2025) — double function of housing, irrational exuberance
- CBR press release (April 2025) — subsequent macroprudential tightening

The report illustrates the policy dilemma: monetary easing supported mortgage lending but also fueled housing price growth, requiring subsequent macroprudential tightening.

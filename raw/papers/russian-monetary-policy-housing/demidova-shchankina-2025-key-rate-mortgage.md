# Impact of key interest rate changes on mortgage rates in Russian regions

**Авторы:** О.А. Demidova, А.А. Shchankina (Национальный исследовательский университет "Высшая школа экономики")  
**Журнал:** Экономика и математические методы (РАН), 2025, Том 61, № 2, С. 75-89  
**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.31857/S0424738825020061

## Abstract

The article considers the interest rate channel of the monetary policy transmission of the Bank of Russia to the weighted average mortgage rate published on the website of the Bank of Russia and the "commercial" mortgage rate calculated by the authors less mortgage programmes: preferential, family, Far East, Arctic and mortgage programme for IT specialists.

## Key Findings

1. **Before COVID-19 (Jan 2016 – Feb 2020):** Long-term relationship of mortgage rates with MIACR (Moscow Interbank Actual Credit Rate) and adjustment to long-term equilibrium found in:
   - **76 regions** for weighted average mortgage rates
   - **61 regions** for commercial mortgage rates

2. **COVID-19 impact (Jan 2016 – Feb 2022):** Impacted transmission of both weighted average and commercial rates approximately equally. Long-term relationship remained in:
   - **4 regions** for weighted average rates
   - **14 regions** for commercial rates

3. **SMO shock (Jan 2016 – Aug 2023):** Stronger influenced transmission of weighted average rates than commercial rates. Long-term relationship remained in:
   - **5 regions** for weighted average rates
   - **30 regions** for commercial rates

4. **Regional timing differences:** Before COVID-19, in European part of Russia, weighted average mortgage rates reacted to MIACR changes mainly in the month after the shock. In Siberia and Far East — in the next month, which may be caused by multiple intermediaries.

## Methodology

- Data: 85 Russian regions, monthly data, January 2016 – August 2023
- Method: Error Correction Model (ECM)
- Two dependent variables:
  - Weighted average mortgage rate (published by Bank of Russia)
  - "Commercial" mortgage rate (calculated by authors, excluding subsidized programmes: preferential, family, Far East, Arctic, IT specialists)

## Key Interpretation

The dramatic reduction in regions with long-term equilibrium relationship (from 76 to 4-5 for weighted average, from 61 to 14-30 for commercial) indicates that:
- **COVID-19 and SMO shocks broke the monetary transmission mechanism** for mortgage rates in most regions
- Subsidized lending programmes (льготная ипотека) may have weakened the link between key rate and actual mortgage rates
- Commercial mortgage rates (excluding subsidies) maintained transmission better than weighted average rates (which include subsidies)

## Relevance

This paper directly addresses the question: **Does the Bank of Russia's key rate effectively transmit to mortgage rates across Russian regions?**

Key implication: If monetary transmission is broken in most regions (as results suggest), monetary policy may be **ineffective** at cooling housing demand through rate hikes, especially when subsidized lending programmes are active.

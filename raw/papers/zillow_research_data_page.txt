Zillow Research Data Portal

Source URL: https://www.zillow.com/research/data/
Access Status: BLOCKED (CloudFront 403 / PerimeterX bot protection)

Content Expected (from public knowledge and FRED integration):
- ZHVI (Zillow Home Value Index) - national, state, metro, county, ZIP, neighborhood
- ZORI (Zillow Observed Rent Index) - rental price indices
- ZHVF (Zillow Home Value Forecast) - forward-looking projections
- Market reports: inventory, days on market, sale-to-list ratio, price cuts
- Affordability metrics: mortgage payment share, down payment, income ratios
- Rental market: median rent, rent growth, vacancy
- New construction: permits, starts, completions
- Demographics: migration, household formation
- Methodology documentation: ZHVI user guide, ZHVI methodology (2019 highlights + deep dive)

ZHVI User Guide: https://www.zillow.com/research/zhvi-user-guide/
